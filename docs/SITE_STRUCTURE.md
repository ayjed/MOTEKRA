## Supporting routes

| Route | Required content / publication policy |
|---|---|
| `/work/[slug]` | Approved project evidence, context, exact ownership/contribution, constraints, process, solution and verified outcomes only. Source screenshots alone do not establish a full case study |
| `/services/[slug]` | Confirmed offering, useful deliverables, FAQs, related approved work and inquiry CTA; never fabricate case links |
| `/team/[slug]` | Approved publishing name/title, genuine biography, portrait if supplied, verified profile URLs and correctly attributed independent work. Defer public slug until name confirmation |
| `/insights` and `/insights/[slug]` | Genuine reviewed editorial content with actual author/date. Keep out of public discovery until content exists |
| `/privacy`, `/terms` where applicable | Owner-reviewed policies reflecting actual form processing and tracking; no invented legal assurances |
| Not-found and error states | Accessible recovery links and readable content independent of motion |
| `/sitemap.xml`, `/robots.txt` | Approved public URLs only; exclude unpublished records and staging. Canonical origin awaits domain confirmation |

## Rendering and attribution contract

Server Components render headings, descriptions, project/service links and CTAs. Client boundaries own GSAP/ScrollTrigger, lazy R3F visuals and Motion microinteractions. Canvas is decorative; static posters, reduced-motion, mobile and error states preserve the same content/actions. No scroll hijacking or animation-dependent visibility.

Company projects and founder-independent projects share the Work catalog but retain distinct labels and contributor fields. Employment at a company does not make that company's project a MOTEKRA engagement. Collaborations identify the contribution without claiming sole ownership. Concept and delivery status are separate from ownership and approval.

The reference's partner strip, testimonial avatars, named mockup projects, founder image, contact details and performance/experience figures are excluded as factual inputs. Main-frame section balance and product-centered visual language are retained.
