Method / a proposed approach

Make the decisions visible.

  1. 01

    Understand the task

    Review the product context, intended users, existing evidence and constraints. Identify what needs validation rather than assuming the answer.

  2. 02

    Shape the flow

    Map the important journeys and content hierarchy. Explore alternative layouts before committing to detailed screens.

  3. 03

    Make it testable

    Connect the selected states into a prototype, review feedback and document decisions for implementation. Research and testing depth would be agreed in scope.

Questions / before the brief

A clearer starting point.

Is user research included?+

Research depth is a scope decision. Existing research, stakeholder input, interviews or usability sessions can inform the work, but no research activity is promised without agreement.

Can this build on an existing design system?+

An existing system can be the starting point. Component reuse, missing states, accessibility requirements and handoff expectations should be reviewed before proposing new patterns.

Does a prototype mean the product is built?+

No. A prototype communicates intended behavior. Production implementation, integrations and testing need their own agreed scope.
