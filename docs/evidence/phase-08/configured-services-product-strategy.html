Method / a proposed approach

Make the decisions visible.

  1. 01

    Frame the question

    Gather the available evidence, stakeholders and constraints. Distinguish known needs from assumptions that still require investigation.

  2. 02

    Choose what matters

    Compare options against the agreed goals and constraints. Record why a capability belongs in the first scope or can wait.

  3. 03

    Plan the next decision

    Organize the work into reviewable stages. Identify dependencies and the evidence needed to proceed, without presenting estimates as guarantees.

Questions / before the brief

A clearer starting point.

Will this validate an idea?+

It can make assumptions and validation needs clearer. A strategy document alone does not prove demand, commercial viability or product-market fit.

Is the roadmap a fixed delivery schedule?+

No. Timing depends on the agreed scope, team, dependencies and evidence. The proposed roadmap uses decision checkpoints rather than invented dates.

Can strategy happen before design or development?+

Yes, as a proposed planning activity. The useful output is a shared brief and a reasoned next step; implementation is a separate scope decision.
